# Project brief: seomcp.de

## Accepted purpose

seomcp.de is the concise technical operating surface for a future Contextter MCP service. It is
reserved for a real endpoint, connection and authorization flow, machine-readable metadata,
security information, and externally verified status. Until those systems exist and pass their
gates, the site remains an explicit pre-launch project surface.

Indexable education, server comparisons, compatibility tests, and implementation guidance belong
on the separately owned seo-mcp.de. Shared ownership is disclosed and must never be presented as
independent corroboration.

## Audience

- MCP client users looking for the canonical Contextter service location.
- Developers evaluating a future connection.
- Operations and security reviewers checking scope, ownership, and release gates.

## Visual thesis

A calm, modern European B2B product surface rather than a technical console: true white space,
deep navy typography, one restrained cobalt accent, cool-blue information bands, thin rules, and
precise editorial layouts. The interface uses an open connection diagram, a service ledger, and
an architectural domain split to explain the plan without terminal motifs, fake dashboards,
fake metrics, or speculative product claims.

## Information architecture

- / — concise service plan, operating boundaries, launch gates, and Contextter relationship.
- /security — current security boundary and private disclosure route.
- /impressum — verified operator and contact details.
- /datenschutz — privacy information for the actual static Vercel deployment.
- /.well-known/security.txt — machine-readable disclosure route.
- /404/ — explicit missing-path response with no blanket redirect.

No service, authorization, or status subdomain is published or claimed before its real deployment.

## Non-goals

- No article archive or evaluation directory for other SEO MCP servers.
- No endpoint, uptime, authorization, security, version, limit, or capability claim before proof.
- No unlicensed third-party text, identity, media, code, data, or endorsements.
- No suggestion that seomcp.de, seo-mcp.de, or another commonly owned property independently
  validates Contextter.

## Evidence boundaries

The domain is owner-confirmed as a newly registered domain and assigned to Contextter in the
canonical DomainPortfolio. No expired-domain, former-operator, archive, historical-rights, or
brand-continuation gate applies. Current Contextter product
copy is limited to its verified public positioning around Keyword Database, Site Audit, and
workspaces. Unknown service-owner, canonical endpoint, auth, status, and incident details remain
unknown.

## Launch gates

1. Keep meta robots and X-Robots-Tag at noindex until explicit launch approval. Keep the public preview crawlable through `robots.txt` so crawlers can observe the exclusion.
2. Select a canonical endpoint and named service/security owners.
3. Verify tenant isolation, scopes, authorization, limits, cost controls, audit, privacy, and
   incident handling.
4. Deploy and externally test the real service, metadata, security, and status surfaces.
5. Record production evidence in DomainPortfolio before removing noindex.
